The camera tracks body movement to control the character, and the LLM builds each next level from your previous run. Buttons and touch map to skills and power-ups.

LLM role: game director. It reads run telemetry, such as where you died and which move you kept missing, then authors the next level with reasons, plus personalized missions.

Laptop cam: continuous pose tracking for jump, duck
Push button: activates a skill.
Touch sensor: triggers a power-up.
Countdown display: level time limit.
Buzzer and LED: collision, power-up ready.
